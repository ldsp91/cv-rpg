# Art pack: Kenney 2015 "Roguelike" series; world named Tech City

Context: v1 needs pixel art for a public portfolio site — free license only (VISION's no list), 16×16 tiles on the 320×240 canvas, a human protagonist (the owner), indoor locations (school, university, offices), and a modern-city world theme. "Art research: free pixel-art tileset + character candidates" verified two candidates; the owner kept the pick (their aesthetic call, "pixel arty" standing preference) and settled the naming with it.

We decided: Kenney's 2015 "Roguelike" series — the Roguelike/RPG pack (outdoors + indoor rooms), the Roguelike Modern City pack (roads, shopfronts, office buildings), and Roguelike Characters (composable humans) — all CC0 1.0, all 16×16 on a 1px-margin grid, with a one-line footer credit ("Art: Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0"). The world is named **Tech City** (the Career file's `world` field), and the naming convention is themed world, real locations: Locations keep their real titles, shared with the CV derived from the same data, and the theme rides on the world name, the tile art, and NPC dialogue.

Why: it is the only verified candidate with composable human characters — the player is the owner, and NPC dialogue needs people; its indoor room tiles fit the career locations, and its city pack fits the world theme directly. Its trade-off is the muted, earthy 2015 palette, which the owner accepted.

Considered options:
- **Kenney "Tiny" series** (Tiny Town + Tiny Dungeon + Tiny Creatures, 2022–2024): the more vivid "pixel arty" look, but no human sprites (monsters/animals only) and no modern-city coverage; mixing in a third human pack would break the single cohesive style the research required.
- DawnLike (contradictory license mentions — disqualifying for a public site), Spelunky-inspired sidescroll tilesets (wrong projection for a top-down map), Pixelwood Valley (free tier, no verifiable permissive license), LPC 32×32 (wrong tile size for the canvas, CC-BY-SA).

Consequences: all world art is built from the three 2015 packs — switching packs or theme later is a full re-texture and re-theme. No verified CC0 16×16 pack carries walk frames, so characters are single-tile: free pixel movement, at most a 2-frame bob.
